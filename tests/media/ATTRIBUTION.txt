Source: LibriSpeech ASR corpus, test-clean subset (utterance IDs in the file names).
LibriSpeech ships 16 kHz FLAC. These are the 10-second, 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit PCM
versions used in the paper's experiments.

  V. Panayotov, G. Chen, D. Povey and S. Khudanpur,
  "LibriSpeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books,"
  Proc. IEEE ICASSP 2015, pp. 5206-5210.
  https://www.openslr.org/12

The recordings come from LibriVox public domain audiobooks. The demo plays them,
watermarks them in the browser and runs attacks on them. The files here are unmodified
copies of those experiment clips.
